## Overall Impression

Built by someone with real taste, losing to itself in one specific way: designed to be admired, not to convert. Craft concentrated in moments; evaporates across the two surfaces carrying the argument — the 96-item archive that is 90% of the homepage, and the end of every article. Biggest opportunity is not a redesign: the exits are already built and simply aren't rendered. `_layouts/post.html` computes data-post-prev/next correctly; only the j/k handler consumes them.

## What's Working

1. The search overlay is genuinely excellent interaction design — seeds real queries, shows recents, labels kind, highlights in title and snippet, traps and restores focus, lazy-loads. It is the only place the taxonomy is visible to the reader.
2. The design tokens are a real system, and dark mode proves it — re-authored, not inverted, with the reason stated inline. The site is evidence for its own argument.
3. Journey's rhetorical structure is correct: claim (four numbered capabilities) separated from proof (hero case study + outcome), with card facts pulled from each case study's front matter so they cannot drift. The career logo strip answers "is this person legit" in two seconds.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Every post is a dead end, and the exit data is already in the DOM
Why: the most common arrival is a deep link. That visitor ends on an empty giscus frame — no next read, no author, no route to the work, no signup. newsletter-signup.html is gated behind the Newsletter category, so blog readers never see it.
Fix: render prev/next as titled links from the existing attributes; add a two-line author card; move the signup outside the conditional; give case studies a next story and the email CTA.
Command: /impeccable shape

### [P1] Three job titles for one role, four names for one destination, /journey/ 404s
Why: _config.yml "lead product designer" / _data/journey.yml "Lead Experience Designer" / _pages/about.md "lead designer" — the three pages a hiring manager reads in sequence. Nav "Journey", H1 "Journey", URL /portfolio/, footer "Back to the portfolio", and /journey/ 404s. On a site claiming "I build coherence", this is a counter-argument.
Fix: single source of truth in _config.yml; one destination name; /journey/ redirect (jekyll-redirect-from installed); strip ⊂ in the case-study template; fix the double space left by `replace: "@", ""`.
Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] Half the interface is behind :hover — the primary CTA is invisible on touch
Why: the @media (hover: none) block resets .jcard padding-right and hides .jcard__hint but never touches .jcard__cue or the 2.9rem bottom padding. On mobile each key case study has 46px of dead reserved space and no tappable affordance. The Journey rail renders unlabelled numerals. Mobile is where a recruiter opens a link.
Fix: persistent cue under hover:none; label the rail with area headlines; permanent quiet state for year counts.
Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P2] The 96-entry feed has no hierarchy, no kind, no curation — on the largest surface
Why: 5,137px desktop / 7,088px (8.4 screens) mobile, all identical weight. A first-timer cannot tell a flagship essay from a Tuesday note. The lede promises a "Design explorer" who explores nothing with the reader.
Fix: (a) a "Start here" block of 3-5 picks with a reason each; (b) encode kind in the date gutter, lifting e51 out of the title string; (c) collapse pre-2024 years behind disclosure.
Command: /impeccable layout

### [P2] The topic chips are the weakest control — both assessments found it independently
Why: strongest convergence of the run. A (blind): reads as disabled — muted grey on grey fill with grey border, the universal disabled treatment. B (measured): #ef4444 on #1f1f1f = 4.38:1 at 13px, failing AA in dark; and `.is-active` uses the same failing pair as a PERSISTENT state, invisible to the static scan because topics.js adds it at runtime. Payoff also fails: #discovery yields 14 entries across 8 year headers, and no URL to share.
Fix: raise rest-state contrast; fix the accent-on-surface pair in dark; push active chip to ?topic= via replaceState with popstate; drop year grouping when filtered.
Command: /impeccable colorize, then /impeccable harden
