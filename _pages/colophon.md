This site is hand-built and deliberately small.

**Type** is [IBM Plex Mono](https://www.ibm.com/plex/), all the way down. The monospace grid is the backbone everything else lines up to.

**Colour** is black, red, and white, a [Bauhaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bauhaus) palette, where red is rationed to the few things that have earned your attention.

**Built with** [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted on GitHub Pages. No theme, no framework, no analytics, no cookie banner. The stylesheet and scripts together come to about 35 KB, less than a single photo.

**Under the hood** is plain CSS and a few small vanilla scripts: full-text search, a dark mode that follows your system, and a handful of quiet details for anyone paying attention. (Press <kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> to walk the writing, <kbd>/</kbd> to search.)

The source is [on GitHub](https://github.com/polgarp/polgarp.github.io).
